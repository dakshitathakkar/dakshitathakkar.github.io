04 / ABOUT

A little more
about me.

I care about what it feels like to work with a system. Can the next engineer follow the code? Does a trace help someone understand a failure? Will an abstraction still make sense when the original author isn’t around to explain it? Those questions influence the choices I make.

I enjoy moving between the implementation and the bigger picture. Sometimes the useful work is in a query; sometimes it’s in deciding where a responsibility belongs. I like being close enough to the details to see the difference.

Outside the code, I value conversations where people share the reasoning behind a design, including the constraints and compromises. Attending the MCP Developers Summit and KubeCon India brought me into more of those conversations. They’re a big part of what I enjoy about Bengaluru’s developer community.
